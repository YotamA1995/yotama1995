Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tiny Tale?

Tiny Tale is a mobile app that transforms your child's everyday photos into personalized bedtime stories and poems. Simply upload a photo, add a few details about your child, and receive a magical story in minutes.

How does it work?

You add a photo or a short note, choose a vibe (Bed time or Enerjetic), and Tiny Tale creates a 5-page, read-aloud friendly mini-book with a matching illustration style.

What ages is it for?

Best for ages 0-7. Text is simple, rhythmic, and bedtime-friendly.

Is my child's data and photos safe?

Yes. Your content is encrypted in transit and at rest. You control what you upload and can delete content anytime from the app.

Do you train AI on our photos?

No. We do not use your private content to train our models.

Can I edit the story?

Yes. You can tweak names, tone, and small details before saving or sharing.

What languages are supported?

English at launch, with Hebrew and more coming soon.

Can I print or share the story?

Yes. Save as PDF for printing, or share a link/video from the app.

What does it cost?

You can try a few stories for free. After that, choose a subscription or buy credits (pricing shown in app).

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Subscriptions can be canceled at any time in your app store settings.

I need help - how do I contact you?

In the app, go to Settings -> Help, or email support@tinytaleqpp.com
