Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tiny Tale?

Tiny Tale is a mobile app that transforms your child's everyday photos into personalized bedtime stories and poems. Simply upload a photo, add a few details about your child, and receive a magical story in minutes.

Is it safe for kids' photos?

Absolutely. We prioritize your child's privacy and safety. Photos are processed locally on your device whenever possible, and we never store or share personal images. All stories are generated using secure, privacy-focused technology.

How long does it take to get a story?

Most stories are generated in under 2 minutes. The process includes analyzing your photo, understanding your child's details, and creating a personalized story with beautiful illustrations.

Will it work in Hebrew and English?

Yes! Tiny Tale supports multiple languages including Hebrew and English. You can choose your preferred language for both the app interface and the generated stories.

How much will it cost?

We're still finalizing our pricing, but we plan to offer both free stories (with limitations) and affordable subscription options for unlimited stories. Early access users will get special pricing when we launch.
